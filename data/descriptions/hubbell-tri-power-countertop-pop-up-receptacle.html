This innovative power solution combines traditional AC outlets with cutting-edge USB-C and USB-A charging ports, plus integrated wireless Qi charging—all concealed beneath a sleek, spring-loaded lid that stays hidden until you need it. Designed for the Way You Live and Work

Whether you're powering kitchen appliances during meal preparation, charging multiple mobile devices in a home office, or creating a clutter-free workspace, the Tri-Power outlet adapts to your lifestyle. The manual spring-lift mechanism deploys smoothly with a gentle press, revealing all charging options exactly when needed. When not in use, the unit retracts flush with your countertop surface, preserving clean sightlines and protecting the receptacle from dust and debris. The spill-sealed lid provides exceptional water resistance, engineered to handle countertop spills without compromising electrical safety—a critical feature for kitchen islands, bathroom vanities, and coffee stations where moisture exposure is inevitable.

Available in seven sophisticated finishes including White, Black, Nickel, Chrome, Aluminum, Brass, and Bronze, this countertop outlet integrates seamlessly with your existing décor and hardware. Choose between 15-amp and 20-amp configurations to match your specific power requirements, with the 20-amp option ideal for high-draw appliances and professional workspace applications.

Installation & Setup

The Hubbell Tri-Power features a compact footprint designed for straightforward surface-mount installation on granite, quartz, marble, butcher block, and solid-surface countertops. The space-saving design requires minimal under-cabinet clearance, making it ideal for even shallow cabinetry. Installation requires a standard countertop cutout and connection to a dedicated 120V circuit. For proper compliance and warranty coverage, professional installation by a licensed electrician is recommended. Once installed, this UL-listed receptacle provides years of reliable service, meeting all NEC requirements for countertop work surfaces and seating areas while delivering modern charging convenience that keeps pace with today's connected lifestyle.
