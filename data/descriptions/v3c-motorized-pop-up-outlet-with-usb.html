Installation & Setup

Installing the V3C kitchen countertop outlet is straightforward for DIY enthusiasts or professional installers. The unit requires a 3¾-inch diameter cutout in your countertop surface and adequate clearance underneath—typically 6 to 8 inches of cavity space. Many homeowners position these pop up power outlets between cabinetry under kitchen islands or behind workspace drawers. The V3C connects to a standard under-counter wall outlet, eliminating the need for hardwiring. Once installed, the flush-mount design disappears completely into your surface, preserving your countertop's sleek appearance until power is needed.
