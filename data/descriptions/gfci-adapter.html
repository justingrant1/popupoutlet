Intelligent Protection Where You Need It Most

Kitchen countertop outlets face unique challenges—moisture from sinks, wet hands, and splashing liquids create potential ground fault hazards. This GFCI adapter outlet installs directly between your pop-up outlet and the wall socket beneath your countertop, continuously monitoring electrical current for dangerous imbalances. When it detects a ground fault condition, it immediately interrupts power to prevent shock hazards. What sets this adapter apart is its automatic reset capability: after tripping, simply cut power to the circuit for ten seconds, and the device automatically restores protection when electricity returns. This intelligent feature proves invaluable when your pop-up outlet sits in hard-to-access locations behind drawers or deep under countertops, eliminating the need to crawl underneath and manually press reset buttons.

Installation & Setup

Installing your GFCI adapter outlet requires no special tools or electrical expertise. Simply plug the adapter into the existing wall socket located beneath your countertop, then connect your pop-up outlet's power cord to the adapter's receptacle. The device immediately begins monitoring for ground faults. For optimal safety, verify proper operation by pressing the test button after installation. Remember that this adapter is engineered exclusively for indoor applications and should never be used outdoors or in weather-exposed locations.
