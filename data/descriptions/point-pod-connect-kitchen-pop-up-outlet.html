Invisible Power, Elevated Design

The Point Pod Connect Kitchen Counter Pop Up Outlet redefines how power integrates into luxury kitchen spaces. Engineered for homeowners who refuse to compromise between functionality and aesthetics, this motorized pop-up outlet disappears completely into your countertop when not in use. By allowing you to inlay your actual countertop material into the unit's top surface, the Point Pod Connect achieves a seamless, virtually invisible installation that preserves the uninterrupted beauty of marble, granite, quartz, or any premium surface.

With two standard US outlets plus USB-C and USB-A charging ports, this kitchen countertop outlet delivers flexible power exactly where you need it—without the visual clutter of traditional wall outlets or bulky power strips. Simply press the top and watch as the motorized lift mechanism smoothly raises the outlet assembly, bringing power to your workspace. When finished, press again to retract it flush with your counter. It's the perfect solution for powering blenders, stand mixers, coffee makers, and charging phones, tablets, and laptops while maintaining the clean lines of a high-end kitchen.

Key Features

  • Motorized lift mechanism with press-to-activate operation for effortless access
  • Customizable top surface accepts your own countertop material for seamless integration
  • Two grounded US power outlets rated for 15A kitchen appliances
  • USB-C and USB-A charging ports available in 15W or 65W configurations
  • UL certified for kitchen installation (ETL Listed, UL 962A & CSA C22.2)
  • Available in black or silver finish to complement any cabinetry
  • Compact 4-inch diameter footprint fits standard countertop cutouts

Choose Your Charging Power

The Point Pod Connect offers two USB power options to match your household needs. The 15W configuration includes three USB ports (two USB-C and one USB-A), ideal for charging smartphones and smaller electronics throughout the day. The 65W version features two high-power USB-C ports that charge laptops and tablets at full speed while rapidly powering phones—perfect for households with multiple power-hungry devices.

Installation & Setup

Installation requires a 4-inch diameter hole in your kitchen countertop. The unit ships with a blank top panel, allowing you to cut and insert your own countertop material for a truly custom fit that matches your existing surface perfectly. This integrated approach makes the Point Pod Connect the premier choice for designers, architects, and homeowners creating sophisticated kitchen environments where every detail matters.
