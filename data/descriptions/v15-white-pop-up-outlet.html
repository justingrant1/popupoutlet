Installation & Setup

Installing your V15 pop up outlet is straightforward and requires only a standard 80mm (3.15-inch) diameter hole in your countertop, desk, or kitchen island. The integrated mounting flange accommodates minor variations in hole size while ensuring a secure, flush fit with your work surface. The unit arrives ready to wire with clear instructions, and the built-in overload protector—identifiable by a red reset switch on the underside—provides automatic protection against electrical faults. For kitchen installations near sinks or wet areas, we recommend consulting our specialized waterproof models designed specifically for high-moisture environments. The V15 is UL listed, conforming to UL STD. 962A and certified to CSA STD. C22.2 NO.308, ensuring full compliance with North American electrical safety standards.
