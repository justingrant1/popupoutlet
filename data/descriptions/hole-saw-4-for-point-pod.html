Installation & Setup

This hole saw features a 5/8-11 threaded connection designed for professional-grade coring drills or CNC equipment—the proper tools for cutting expensive stone countertops. It does not include a standard drill arbor adapter, and we strongly recommend against attempting to use one with handheld drills. Improper equipment can result in chipped edges, cracked surfaces, or irreparable damage to your kitchen countertop, especially on materials like quartz and granite.

For optimal results when installing your Point Pod kitchen power outlet, use appropriate professional coring equipment operated at the correct speed and feed rate for your specific countertop material. This ensures clean entry and exit points, preserving the structural integrity of your workspace surface while creating a perfectly sized opening for your pop-up charging station.
