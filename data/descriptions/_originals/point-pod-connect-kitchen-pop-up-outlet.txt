The Point Pod Connect is an integrated kitchen counter pop up outlet system. As a result, it offers seamless integration of your countertop material in the top of the unit. Featuring USB ports and two US sockets for kitchen appliances and mobile devices.

Its aesthetic design allows it to match your countertop material. Note, the top comes blank without a material - you need to cut and insert your own bench material. Watch the installation video below for more information. 

The Point Pod rises when you press the top and electric motor moves it up and down for easy access.

The Point Pod kitchen counter pop up outlet is UL certified and approved for kitchens in North America (ETL Listed, UL 962A &amp; CSA C22.2). Its rated for up to 15A. 

The Point Pod Connect is the only kitchen pop up model where you put your countertop stone cut-out back in the top for a seamless fitting. It is the top of the range unit available at Modern Power Solutions, designed for high end homes. 

The Point Pod is available in both black (PPCV815B) and silver-white (PPCV815) colour variations. 

It also comes in two USB power configurations: 15W or 65W. 

The 15-watt version features three USB ports (two USB-C and one USB-A) making it ideal for charging phones and smaller devices. While it can charge laptops and tablets, they will charge more slowly.

For faster performance, the 65-watt version includes two USB-C ports and is better suited for charging laptops and tablets at normal speed, as well as charging phones more quickly. Please note that this version does not include a USB-A port.

A 4" diameter hole is required for the Point Pod installation in a kitchen countertop.
