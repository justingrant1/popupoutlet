Use a GFCI adapter outlet to enhance safety for pop-up countertop outlets located near wet areas, such as in kitchens. Most modern circuits already include GFCI protection, so this adapter is usually unnecessary. However, if you’re unsure, using it adds an extra layer of protection.

To use the GFCI adapter outlet, connect it between the pop-up outlet and the wall socket under your countertop. If it trips to prevent an electrical hazard, reset it by pressing the GFCI’s reset button located under the countertop.

Alternatively, you can turn off the electrical circuit for 10 seconds. The adapter automatically resets when power is restored, making it especially convenient for hard-to-reach locations like under countertops or behind drawers.

The ELEGRP GFCI adapter outlet supports up to 15 amps of electrical current and is designed for indoor use only. It is not weather-resistant, so avoid using it outdoors. This UL-listed device meets certification standards for homes across North America.
