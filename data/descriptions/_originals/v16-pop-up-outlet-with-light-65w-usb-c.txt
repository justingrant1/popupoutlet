Introducing the V16 Pop-Up Power Outlet from Modern Power Solutions – a premium, space-saving power solution designed for modern kitchens, islands, benchtops, and workspaces.

Engineered with a robust aluminium construction and smooth gas strut operation, the V16 delivers reliable performance, exceptional durability, and a refined user experience. The unit installs neatly into a standard 100mm (4") cut-out, providing discreet access to power when needed while maintaining a clean, uncluttered surface when closed.

The V16 features two power outlets alongside advanced USB charging, including a high-power 65W USB-C port capable of fast charging laptops, tablets, smartphones, and other USB-C devices. A built-in warm white LED light provides subtle ambient illumination and can be dimmed to suit any environment.

Designed for simple installation, the V16 is supplied fully wired with a power cable and secures easily into place using the included locking ring. Its plug-and-play design eliminates complicated wiring, making installation fast and straightforward.

Certified for both US and UK markets, the V16 meets stringent international safety standards and is backed by a 24-month warranty for complete peace of mind.
