4″ Hole Saw Drill Bit with 3.66″ Inner Dimension and 5/8-11 Thread for Point Pod Installation

Overview

Engineered for precision, this 4" (102mm) hole saw features a 3.66″ (93mm) inner dimension and an 5/8-11 thread—perfectly suited for creating accurate cut-outs in stone benchtops when installing a Point Pod. Designed to deliver a clean, seamless fit, it allows the cut-out section to slot neatly into the top of the Point Pod, ensuring a flawless, professional finish.

Please note: This hole saw does not include an arbour adapter for standard drills. It features a 5/8-11 threaded connection, which is designed for professional coring drills or CNC machines.

Because attaching this hole saw to a regular handheld drill can easily damage your countertop - especially for inexperienced users - we strongly advise against using an arbour adapter.

If you do require an arbour adapter, you can find one here or contact us for assistance.
