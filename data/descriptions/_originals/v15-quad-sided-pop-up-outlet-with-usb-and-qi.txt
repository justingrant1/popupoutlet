The V15 Range of Pop Up Outlets is a premium gas-strut operated solution designed for the U.S. market. With a simple press on the top, the outlet smoothly rises for use; push it down again, and it retracts seamlessly back into the countertop. The gas-strut mechanism operates similarly to the height adjustment of an office chair, providing smooth and effortless movement.

Installation is straightforward - the V15 fits into a 3.15” (80mm) diameter hole. A built-in flange allows for slight variations in hole size, ensuring a secure and flush fit.

The V15 range features a seamless, blank top - unlike many competing models that display text or engravings on the lid. Modern Power Solutions has focused on creating a clean, refined aesthetic with design improvements that elevate it above comparable products on the market.

Built for 15-amp power capacity, the V15 includes an integrated overload protector, indicated by a red switch on the underside of the unit. This safety feature automatically activates to prevent overheating, fire hazards, or other electrical risks.
