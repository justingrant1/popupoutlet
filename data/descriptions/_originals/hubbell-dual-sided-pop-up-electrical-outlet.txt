Hubbell Countertop Receptacles are UL-listed pop-up outlets specifically designed for permanent countertop installations. Engineered for use in kitchens, bathrooms, and islands, these receptacles provide a reliable and convenient power source exactly where it’s needed most.

The Hubbell Countertop Receptacles meet UL listing standards, ensuring full compliance with NEC® 406.5(E) for countertops, 406.5(F) for work surfaces, and 406.5(H) for seating areas.

Built for durability and safety, these Pop-Up Receptacles are water-resistant, tested to withstand spills of up to half a gallon. They also feature Hubbell’s patent-pending silicone gaskets and innovative, patented cam-action tamper-resistant technology. The low-profile, space-saving design minimizes impact both on the countertop surface and within the cabinet below, making installation seamless and unobtrusive.
