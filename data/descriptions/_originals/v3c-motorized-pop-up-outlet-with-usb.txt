The V3C is an automatic motorized black pop up outlet built for kitchens. It extracts out of a kitchen counter top or any desktop surface by the press of a button on the top.

Easily charge mobile devices via the USB ports on the face, and power up to two 120v appliances. Once done, simply close the unit and a seal protects against water spills that may occur around on the countertop.

The V3C pop up outlet is easy to install. It requires a 3 3/4 inch diameter hole and plugs into a wall outlet under the counter. However, you'll need to ensure you have a cavity or space under the countertop. For example, often people will fit them between or behind cupboards under an island counter.
